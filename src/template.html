Nothing leaves this device

The pages are read in this tab. There is no server, no upload and no account. The signature stays in this browser, and the document is gone when you close the tab.

What it does not do

  • No sending documents to other people to sign.
  • No certificate based digital signature. This is a simple electronic signature, drawn into the page.
  • No editing of the text that is already in the PDF.
